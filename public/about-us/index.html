About Us

We are Assiut Robotics, the first student team dedicated to robotics at Assiut University's Faculty of Engineering. Since 2009, we've fostered technical growth through workshops, projects, and competing in local and international arenas.

Our Mission

To be a leading team in robotics, equipping members with technical and engineering skills to excel in the job market.

Our Vision

Inspiring the next generation of engineers and makers through practical experience, teamwork, and innovation.
